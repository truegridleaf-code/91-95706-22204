Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our certified pest control treatments across New Delhi.

How quickly can your pest control team arrive in New Delhi? +

We provide same-day and 24/7 emergency response across New Delhi, including Tughalkabad, Saket, Lajpat Nagar, Mehrauli, Badarpur, and Greater Delhi NCR. A certified technician typically reaches your doorstep within 60 to 90 minutes of your booking call.

Are your pest control chemicals safe for children and household pets? +

Yes, 100% safe. We exclusively utilize Govt. of India and WHO-approved, non-toxic, odorless, and third-generation eco-friendly formulations. You do not need to vacate your home for standard cockroach gel treatments or herbal sprays. For heavy chemical treatments, our certified technicians advise simple, practical precautions (such as keeping infants and pets away for 2-3 hours).

What warranty and guarantee do you offer on Termite and Bed Bug treatments? +

We offer up to 1 to 5 years service warranty on our Anti-Termite treatments, and comprehensive guarantees on Bed Bug & Cockroach packages. If any pest activity reoccurs during your warranty tenure, our team conducts free re-treatment with zero extra visit or labor charges.

Do you provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for businesses and restaurants? +

Yes, we offer customized commercial AMC plans tailored for restaurants, hotels, corporate offices, warehouses, clinics, and manufacturing facilities across Delhi. Our AMC includes scheduled monthly/quarterly inspections, digital pest-sighting logs, audit-ready compliance certificates, and 24/7 emergency callout support.

What steps should I take to prepare my home before the treatment? +

For kitchen cockroach gel baiting, minimal prep is needed — simply keep kitchen counters clear of uncovered food and water. For termite or bed bug treatments, our technicians will guide you during inspection to move light furniture 1-2 feet away from walls and cover open drinking water containers.

What payment methods do you accept and are there any hidden fees? +

We follow 100% transparent, upfront pricing with zero hidden charges. You receive a free, no-obligation estimate before work begins. We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Credit/Debit Cards, and Cash upon satisfactory completion of service.
